# 3-Minute Demo Video Script

| Time | Segment | What to show | What to say |
|------|---------|--------------|-------------|
| 0:00-0:15 | Hook | Problem in one frame | "Every day, ___ struggle with ___." |
| 0:15-0:35 | Who it's for | Persona / real scenario | "Meet ___. Today she has to ___." |
| 0:35-1:50 | Live demo | Happy path, end to end, no slides | Narrate actions, not features |
| 1:50-2:20 | Under the hood | Architecture diagram | "We used ___ for ___ because ___." |
| 2:20-2:45 | Impact | Numbers, before/after | "This cuts ___ from ___ to ___." |
| 2:45-3:00 | Close | Logo + repo link | Name + one-line pitch |

## Recording tips
- Record at 1080p, mic close, quiet room
- Zoom into the UI; judges watch on laptops
- Cut every pause longer than 1 second
- Add captions (many judges watch muted)
- Do not exceed the time limit, even by 5 seconds
